How to work this list

  1. Reply now: a customer is waiting, and it's S0 or S1 or under 60 days old. Approve drafts here first.
  2. Needs you: waiting on us, but the answer needs a maintainer call or the draft has low confidence.
  3. Old, reply and close: waited more than 60 days. Lower risk. Approve in a batch.
  4. Close as stale: we spoke last and the author went quiet. Batch close.
  5. The rest are nudges, internal tracking, posted replies, or already closed.

To approve, tell Claude which to post, for example "post #1769 and #2321". To change a draft first, paste your version. Claude rechecks each thread right before posting and stops if anyone replied since this sweep.
