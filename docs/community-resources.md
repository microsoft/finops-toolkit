## The fine print

This page is here to help you find independent help – it's not a Microsoft-run marketplace or partner program. A listing means an organization has shown public evidence of supporting the FinOps toolkit specifically; it isn't a Microsoft endorsement, certification, or partnership, and Microsoft doesn't vet, audit, or guarantee anyone's work. As always, do your own diligence before engaging a provider – ask about their experience, check references, and make sure they're the right fit for your team.

The FinOps toolkit itself is open source and provided as-is, and Microsoft Support doesn't handle support requests for the toolkit. See [Help + support](./help) for community and self-service options, most of which are free.
